EvalRef3D

Record raw model performance on independent holdout subjects.

Validation runs locally in your browser. Only Ground Truth and raw prediction labelmaps go into the Evaluation Request ZIP. Source MRI/image voxels and model weights stay on your computer.

A Model Evaluation Certificate is a self-evaluated research record. It is not a performance guarantee or clinical validation.
