# Apple Demo Dataset Attribution

Apple demo images are adapted from:

Schut DE, Trull AK, Couvée M. *Dataset of CT scans, slice photographs, and visual browning scores of 120 'Kanzi' apples.* Zenodo. <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.8167285>

The source dataset is licensed under the [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Images were selected, cropped, resized to 1000 x 944 pixels, and JPEG-compressed for the SegRef3D demo. The original data providers do not endorse SegRef3D.
